80th Anniversary · 3D Collection

Eight Sony classics, from the 1955 TR-55 radio to the 2003 Cyber-shot DSC-P8, rebuilt as 3D models in real millimetres and dressed in the Sony 80th-anniversary illustration. Each one opens up to show its inside, rebuilt from service manuals and photos. Turn them, light them, play them as a showcase, or make your own GIFs.
