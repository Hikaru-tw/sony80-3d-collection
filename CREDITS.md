## Sources for sizes and inside details

The models were measured against these. The photos and scans themselves are not included here; each model's notes
(the top of `render/products/<id>.js`) say what was taken from where.

- Sony: service manuals for the TPS-L2 and MDR-3L2, WM-F5, D-E350, DSC-P8, KV-1200U / KV-1210U and MDR-7506; product
  photos and brochures
- [radiomuseum.org](https://www.radiomuseum.org/) members' photos (TR-55)
- [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/) teardowns and guides (D-E356CK, DSC-P10)
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) contributors
- [MoDiP](https://www.modip.ac.uk/), the Museum of Design in Plastics (TPS-L2)
- the Smithsonian's [National Museum of American History](https://americanhistory.si.edu/) (KV-1310, TR-55), the
  V&A and the Science Museum Group collections
- [walkman.land](https://walkman.land/), [tapedeck.org](http://www.tapedeck.org/), hifi-wiki.com, stereo2go.com,
  visions4netjournal.com (KV-1210U), diyaudioheaven (MDR-7506), kakaku.com, ITmedia, IEE Japan's heritage record of
  the TR-55, Flickr, and collectors' and shops' photos
- the Internet Archive, ManualsLib and elektrotanya.com, for the service manual scans; patent EP0440424 for the
  shape of the cassette's head area
